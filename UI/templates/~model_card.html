Name:

GordonAI 

Overview:


The GordonAI AI package leverages the mdeberta-v3-base model for sentiment analysis, emotion detection, and fact-checking classification across various languages. This model is fine-tuned on a fact-checking dataset to classify text into Disinformation, Hoax, FakeNews, or TrueNews categories. The mdeberta-v3-base model, an evolution of BERT and RoBERTa, integrates disentangled attention and an enhanced mask decoder, trained on an 80GB dataset. DeBERTa V3 introduces efficiency improvements through ELECTRA-Style pre-training with Gradient Disentangled Embedding Sharing, enhancing performance on Natural Language Understanding (NLU) tasks.

DeBERTa's architecture employs disentangled attention to compute attention weights based on content and relative positions, surpassing human performance on SuperGLUE benchmarks. The Enhanced mask decoder in DeBERTa incorporates absolute word positions for masked language modeling, enhancing prediction accuracy by considering content and position information. The model's virtual adversarial training method enhances generalization, leading to superior performance on various Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks.

GordonAI, powered by the DeBERTa model, excels in fact-checking, sentiment analysis, and NLP tasks, with potential applications in combating misinformation, enhancing language understanding, and improving text classification accuracy. The Enhanced Mask Decoder (EMD) in DeBERTa accounts for absolute word positions, enhancing prediction accuracy by considering both relative and absolute positions. The Scale-Invariant Fine-Tuning (SiFT) algorithm improves training stability by perturbing normalized word embeddings, particularly beneficial for larger DeBERTa models during fine-tuning for downstream NLP tasks.

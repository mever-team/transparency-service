Overview: The DeBERTa model, which is part of GordonAI, focuses on analyzing sentiments, detecting emotions, and classifying facts in various languages. It is built on the mdeberta-v3-base model that has been fine-tuned on a dataset for fact-checking to sort text into categories like Disinformation, Hoax, FakeNews, or TrueNews. DeBERTa's design includes a disentangled attention mechanism and an improved mask decoder, surpassing human performance on SuperGLUE benchmarks and excelling in tasks related to understanding and generating natural language. The disentangled attention mechanism in DeBERTa enhances performance by calculating attention weights based on the content and positions of words, improving different Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks. By using absolute word positions in the mask decoder, DeBERTa enhances predictions when filling masked words in sentences. Its design based on Transformers with stacked blocks and a disentangled attention mechanism leads to superior performance in fact-checking, sentiment analysis, and emotion detection tasks in various languages. Moreover, the Scale-Invariant Fine-Tuning (SiFT) algorithm enhances training stability by adjusting normalized word embeddings, resulting in better performance, especially in larger DeBERTa models. SiFT normalizes word embedding vectors into stochastic vectors before adjusting them, significantly improving the performance of fine-tuned models in NLP tasks. The Enhanced Mask Decoder (EMD) of the model incorporates absolute word positions after Transformer layers, enhancing performance compared to previous methods like BERT. SiFT's normalization of word embeddings improves training stability, particularly beneficial for larger DeBERTa models, ultimately enhancing the model's overall performance. It's worth mentioning that SiFT is specifically used for DeBERTa15B in SuperGLUE tasks in the experiments conducted, with the potential for more extensive research on SiFT in future studies.

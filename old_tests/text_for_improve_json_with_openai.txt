Following common practice [21, 9, 28, 16], we assess our method’s robustness to perturbations typically applied in
social media by applying blurring, cropping, compression, noise addition, and their combination to each evaluation
sample with 0.5 probability (experimental setting directly taken from [11]). The results in table 5 indicate high
robustness against cropping and compression, with minimal performance losses, and a good level of robustness against
blurring and noise addition. Their combination considerably reduces performance but at similar levels to that of current
SotA without applying perturbations

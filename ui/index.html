This service


The Transparency Services for AI-model Cards is for creating, editing, and managing model cards. The service’s goal is two-fold. First, to assist AI model creators to produce transparent AI models by equipping them with tools to create model cards, and second to provide model cards that are comprehensible for both AI-experts and non-experts like journalists, data scientists, and media professionals.

What's a Model Card?


A model card is a short and compact document that provides essential information about a machine learning model. It is intended to improve transparency, communication, and responsible AI usage by documenting key details about the model’s purpose, intended use, possible misuses, performance, limitations, ethical considerations and more.

Why are they important?


Model cards help users and stakeholders to understand the capabilities and boundaries of an AI system. They promote responsible AI by encouraging creators to reflect on their models’ real-world impact.
